Gia Lai Gong Festival Peak Day, 31 October 2026 - A Field Guide Update

The Gong Festival, Wild Sunflower Week and Chu Dang Ya Volcano Festival 2026 opened on the morning of 30 October at the communal house of Ia Gri village, Bien Ho commune. Today, 31 October, is the peak day of the Bien Ho - Chu Dang Ya cluster: a new communal house ceremony in the morning, the Ia Nueng community tourism village launch at 14:00, and the Gia Lai City Trail still running. This guide covers 31 October and 1 November only; the full festival framework is in the 25 October schedule article.

A correction to the 25 October schedule

The 25 October draft put the new communal house celebration at 14:00 on 31 October. Reports from 29-30 October correct that: the morning of 31 October holds the communal house worship ceremony at Ia Gri village, attended by village elders, artisans and villagers, in front of the newly completed communal house. The 14:00 slot on 31 October belongs to the launch of the Ia Nueng community tourism village product. If you planned around the earlier draft, swap those two times. The trail race figure is also updated: about 3,500 domestic and international runners, replacing the earlier estimate of around 10,000 runners recorded in the 24 October niche file.

Today, 31 October: what to see, where

Morning: the new communal house worship ceremony at Ia Gri village, Bien Ho commune. This is a Jrai ritual marking a newly restored communal house, not a stage show. Watch as a guest at a ceremony: stay outside the ritual area, do not walk through the procession, and skip flash photography while the rite is under way.

At 14:00: the launch of the «Ia Nueng Community Tourism Village, Bien Ho commune» product. This matters most for travellers who plan to return to Gia Lai after the festival, since Ia Nueng is being introduced as a new community tourism stop rather than a one-day festival halt.

All day: a market of OCOP products, local specialties and traditional food, plus the «Gia Lai Gong Culture Space» exhibition at Ia Gri village, running through the whole 30 October - 1 November cluster. The gift-shopping shortlist with addresses and reference prices sits on top5gialai.com; this guide does not rank shops or restaurants, following the two-site boundary.

Also all day: the Gia Lai City Trail, «Dream of the Great Forest», running 30 October - 1 November in Bien Ho commune, with distances of 6 km, 12 km, 30 km, 50 km and 70 km. The course crosses highland roads around Bien Ho, so the area is busier than usual today and some road sections may be restricted during race hours. If you are here for the sunflowers rather than the race, go early and ask the marshals on site before turning onto a race road.

Tomorrow, 1 November

Ethnic minority sports take place at the Ia Gri village communal house yard: stick pushing, tug of war and stilt walking. This closes the Bien Ho - Chu Dang Ya cluster. After 1 November the festival enters a gap with no announced events from 2 to 5 November, then moves to central Pleiku from 6 to 8 November, with a street parade on 6 November and Gong Night at 20:00 on 7 November at Dai Doan Ket Square.

Practical notes for today

Cover one cluster per outing, not Bien Ho and central Pleiku in the same morning. Ia Gri village sits at the foot of Chu Dang Ya volcano and is itself a wild sunflower viewing spot, so one visit there combines the ceremony, the market and the flowers. Wild sunflowers bloom from late October, peak in November and run into December, so missing one flower outing today still leaves a window after the festival.

For stays and food, see the companion lists on top5gialai.com: the Pleiku accommodation list by budget (29 October) and the Pleiku pho kho list (25 October). Festival-week room prices and availability move fast; recheck before booking.
