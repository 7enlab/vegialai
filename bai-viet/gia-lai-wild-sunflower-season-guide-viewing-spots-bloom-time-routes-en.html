Gia Lai Wild Sunflower Season Guide: Where to See Them, When They Bloom, and How to Get There

Wild sunflower season in Gia Lai is a season, not a single photo stop. The flowers start colouring the mountainsides in late October, peak in November and last into December. This guide gathers what has been verified about viewing spots, timing and routes - and states plainly what has not been verified, so you do not travel on an invented number.

This is vegialai.com's seasonal guide. Rankings of restaurants and hotels belong to top5gialai.com under the two sites' agreed boundary, so this article names places only where a route needs them and ranks none of them.

When to go

  • Late October: the first blooms appear, mixed with buds. Come now and you also catch the Gong Festival, 30 October-8 November 2026.
  • November: the peak month, according to several Vietnamese travel guides in agreement (source: Gia Lai niche file, 24/10/2026). This is also when the Festival's main events take place.
  • December: flowers remain, thinning towards mid- to late December. If you miss the Festival, this window still works - just expect sparser blooms late in the month.

Late October is when the Central Highlands shift from the rainy season into the dry season, and travel gets easier (source: vietnam.vn, 26/10/2026). Out-of-season rain is still possible, so check the Pleiku forecast before you set out rather than trusting a blanket «dry season, no rain» claim.

Where to see them

1. Chu Dang Ya volcano, Bien Ho commune - the anchor of the whole season

Chu Dang Ya is Gia Lai's best-known wild sunflower spot: yellow flowers cover the slopes of this extinct volcano, with Ia Gri village at its foot. It is also the centre of the Wild Sunflower Week within the 2026 Festival. Go in the morning: early sun shows the yellow at its clearest, and the village is still quiet before tour groups arrive.

In this area you can also visit Ia Gri village (site of the new communal house celebration at 14:00 on 31 October 2026 during the Festival) and the local produce market and gong culture exhibition running 30 October-1 November at Ia Gri village.

2. Bien Ho (T'Nung Lake), Bien Ho commune - pair it with Chu Dang Ya

Bien Ho is in the same commune as Chu Dang Ya, so the two fit naturally into one day: sunflowers at Chu Dang Ya in the morning, then the lake. The crater lake and its centuries-old pine trees give a completely different backdrop from the yellow slopes, so photos from the two stops in one day will not all look the same.

3. Central Pleiku - for the Festival, not for flowers

Central Pleiku (Dai Doan Ket Square, Pleiku Museum, Anh Hung Nup street) hosts the 6-8 November Festival cluster: the street festival on 6 November, Gong Night at 20:00 on 7 November, ethnic cultural exchanges and coffee and OCOP stalls. Keep a separate day for Pleiku if you come during Festival week; do not merge it with a flower day at Chu Dang Ya, because the two clusters differ in character - one is flowers and a volcano, the other is a festival and a town.

4. Roadside flowers - enjoy them when you meet them, do not make a separate trip

Wild sunflowers grow along many roads around Pleiku and Bien Ho. Stop when you see a beautiful stretch, but this guide lists no «top roadside spots» because no verified data exists for individual road sections. A roadside spot is only trustworthy with an on-site photo carrying a date - the same photo standard this site system uses, and the same check readers should apply to any new spot someone recommends.

Suggested routes

One day: Pleiku - Bien Ho - Chu Dang Ya

Morning at Chu Dang Ya for sunflowers and Ia Gri village; afternoon at Bien Ho, then back to Pleiku. This suits travellers with a single day who want the season's anchor spot.

Two days during the Festival: a flower day plus a festival day

Day 1 follows the one-day route above, ideally 30 October-1 November to catch the Bien Ho Festival cluster. Day 2 stays in central Pleiku for the 6-8 November cluster, anchored on Gong Night at 20:00 on 7 November. No Festival events have been announced in the press-conference sources checked for the gap days 2-5 November - a good window for unhurried flower viewing or other spots around Pleiku, but re-check the province's announcements before travelling, as the organisers may add activities.

With sport: Gia Lai City Trail, 31 October-1 November

The Gia Lai City Trail runs 31 October-1 November 2026 around the Bien Ho - Chu Dang Ya area (source: Gia Lai niche file, 24/10/2026). Runners will pass right through the flower region; non-runners can visit to watch and see flowers in the same period, but should avoid the race's peak hours if they want quiet photos.
